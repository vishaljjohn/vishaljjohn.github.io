      When

      6 PM to 7 AM, only on White Flag nights. Open to men, women, and children.

      When it's called

      When the National Weather Service forecasts 32°F or below, counting wind chill. Season: Nov 1 – Mar 31.

      Free bus

      Owensboro Transit gives free rides to the shelter Monday–Friday, 4–6 PM, on White Flag nights. Tell the driver you're going to White Flag. The shelter does not provide rides.

      If Pitino is full

      Other participating shelters take overflow.

      Pets

      Last winter, pets could stay overnight in a heated space on White Flag nights. Ask shelter staff if this is available this year.

      2-minute guide

      Staying safe in the cold

      Stay dry

      • Wet clothes, especially cotton like jeans and sweatshirts, pull heat from your body.
      • Change into dry clothes as soon as you can.

      Wear layers

      • Several thin layers trap heat better than one big coat.
      • Take one off before you sweat.

      Cover your head, hands & feet

      • Keep a spare pair of socks in a plastic bag in your backpack.
      • Swap them when your feet get damp.

      Eat a little, often

      • Your body burns more energy in the cold.
      • No-cook snacks: peanut butter, nuts, trail mix, granola bars, pop-top cans of tuna, beans, or soup.

      Drink water

      • Drink water through the day, even if you're not thirsty.
      • Warm soup or warm drinks help.

      About alcohol

      • It can make you feel warm, but your body actually loses heat faster.
      • On cold nights, try to stay inside or with someone you trust.

      Frostbite

      Signs

      • Numb fingers, toes, ears, or nose
      • Skin turns white, gray, or waxy

      What to do

      • Warm slowly: armpits, or warm (not hot) water.
      • Don't rub it, and don't hold it to a fire or space heater. Numb skin can burn without you feeling it.

      Hypothermia

      Signs

      • Hard shivering, clumsy hands
      • Confusion, slurred speech, very sleepy

      What to do

      • Get inside.
      • Get out of wet clothes.
      • Warm the chest first, with blankets.

      Call 911 if someone is confused, very sleepy, or was shivering and then stopped. The emergency room (ER) treats everyone, with or without insurance.
